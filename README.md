# ✈️ Content Engagement & Airline Ticket Purchase Analysis

This project explores how user engagement behaviors—such as likes, check-ins, and following a company page—impact airline ticket purchases.  
The ultimate goal is to optimize **ROI on digital marketing campaigns** by uncovering actionable behavioral insights.

---

## 🧪 Project Highlights

- Cleaned and validated real-world engagement data, addressing **data quality** and detecting **anomalies**.
- Performed **exploratory data analysis (EDA)** to identify trends, correlations, and key user segments.
- Provided insight-driven recommendations to enhance targeting and improve **marketing ROI**.

---

## ❓ Business Questions Answered

- Why are most users not purchasing tickets from the airline?
- How do age and employment status influence ticket purchasing behavior?
- What devices are users most active on, and how does device usage relate to conversions?
- Which destinations drive more user interest?
- Does following the company page affect engagement or purchasing behavior?
- How much time do users spend engaging with travel content, and does that relate to conversion?
- What user demographics show the highest likelihood of converting?

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Most users **have not purchased** tickets, despite nearly all having traveled at least once annually.
- **15–35 age group** dominates the user base (~80%) and also forms the majority of ticket purchasers.
- **Non-working users** (85% of base) are more likely to engage but may be price-sensitive.
- **Mobile** is the most-used device; however, **tablet/laptop users** comment more.
- **Beach** and **Financial** (business) destinations are most preferred.
- **70% of users** are not following the company page, yet many still engage with content.
- **Higher time spent** on travel pages **does not always** correlate with purchase intent.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- **Increase Brand Awareness:** Users are traveling but not booking tickets. Run targeted campaigns to boost visibility and trust.
  
- **Optimize Mobile Experience:** Mobile users engage less. Improve mobile UI/UX to increase interactions and conversions.
  
- **Target Young, Non-working Users:** Focus on affordable promotions for the 15-35 age group, which has the highest potential for conversions.

- **Promote Popular Destinations:** Focus marketing on beach and business destinations, as users prefer these locations.

- **Review Pricing Strategy:** Low ticket purchases may indicate pricing issues. Assess if the price is positioned competitively.
